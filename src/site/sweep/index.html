How the gates are scored

  1. point in time → a fixed share of trials per universe is killed for look ahead, because a share of any real sweep leaks restated data
  2. walk forward → the in sample Sharpe is shrunk to a fraction between 30 and 80 percent; anything under 0.45 out of sample dies
  3. costs → gross edge per round trip is the horizon’s edge scale times the walk forward Sharpe; the universe’s round trip cost is subtracted; nothing left, dead
  4. capacity → the universe’s base capacity times the horizon multiplier times a draw; under 40 million dollars, dead
  5. count → the haircut is 0.065 times the square root of twice the natural log of N; at N equals 48 that is 0.18, which is how 0.81 becomes 0.63

The constant 0.065 is illustrative and was chosen so the canonical candidate reproduces. Everything else is arithmetic you can check.
